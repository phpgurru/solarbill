Reading your bill

How do I read a net-metering bill?

Find the four meter registers: import off-peak, import peak, export off-peak and export peak. Imports minus exports in each band give net units. Net units (plus any carried-forward units at settlement) are priced to make the cost of electricity, then the fixed charge and taxes are added. CR after an amount means credit.

What are import and export units?

Import units are electricity you took from the grid, mostly at night and in the evening. Export units are surplus solar your panels sent to the grid in the day. The green (bidirectional) meter records both, split into peak and off-peak.

What do IMP-OP, IMP-PK, EXP-OP and EXP-PK mean?

They are the four registers in the net-metering QR code: import off-peak, import peak, export off-peak and export peak, in kWh (units).

What does a minus sign in net units mean?

You exported more than you imported in that band. Negative net units are credited, not charged.

What does CR mean on my electricity bill?

Credit. An amount ending in CR is owed to you and is adjusted against future bills. There is nothing to pay on a bill that ends in CR.

What is COE on my bill?

Cost of electricity: net units priced at the peak rate and the off-peak or buyback rate, before the fixed charge and taxes. In a settlement month it covers the whole cycle.

Why are my billed units much lower than my imports?

Billed units are net units: imports minus exports across both bands. A home that imported 1,198 units and exported 919 shows 279 billed units.

What do the two QR codes on my bill contain?

One is a payment QR for banking apps, with the DISCO, your consumer ID, reference number, bill month and due date. The other, on net-metering bills, holds the four registers, net units, month count, carried-forward units and the bill components.

What is the tariff code A-1b(03)T?

A-1 is the domestic tariff, b is the time-of-use (peak/off-peak) version for loads of 5 kW and above, and T indicates a three-phase connection.

What is MDI on my bill?

Maximum demand indicator: the highest load in kW your connection drew during the month. For time-of-use consumers it can set the fixed charge, if it is above half your sanctioned load.

Why the bill is high

Why did I get a bill when I exported more units than I imported?

Peak and off-peak are netted separately. Exports are almost all off-peak and a surplus is bought back at Rs 25.32, while evening imports are charged at the peak rate of Rs 46.85. The fixed charge, adjustments and taxes are added regardless. A unit surplus can still be a rupee bill.

Read: Why your net-metering bill is still high, even after exporting more
Why are my peak units not adjusted against my solar export?

Under net metering, peak exports are netted only against peak imports, and off-peak only against off-peak. Panels export almost nothing in the evening peak, so peak imports are billed in full.

Read: Why your net-metering bill is still high, even after exporting more
Why is my bill higher in winter with solar?

Shorter days, smog and a low sun cut exports, while peak hours start earlier (5 PM). Credit built up in summer is drawn down.

Why is every third bill so much bigger?

That is the settlement month (MONTH COUNT 3/3). Peak units carried from the previous two months are priced all at once.

Why did my solar bill go up after March 2026?

NEPRA introduced fixed charges per kW from February 2026 (Rs 675/kW for time-of-use connections), and quarterly and fuel adjustments also change from month to month.

Will a battery reduce my net-metering bill?

Yes, if it moves daytime solar into the evening peak, replacing the most expensive units on the bill. Whether it pays back depends on its cost per usable kWh over its lifetime.

Peak hours

What are peak hours in Pakistan?

A four-hour evening window on PITC DISCOs: 5–9 PM in December to February, 6–10 PM in March to May, 7–11 PM in June to August, and 6–10 PM in September to November.

What are LESCO peak hours right now?

In October and November, 6 PM to 10 PM. From December to February, 5 PM to 9 PM.

What is the peak and off-peak rate per unit in 2026?

Rs 46.85 peak and Rs 34.53 off-peak for domestic time-of-use consumers, before adjustments and tax.

How much do I save by moving load out of peak hours?

For a solar home with daytime surplus, about Rs 25 per unit moved into daylight after tax; avoiding a peak unit altogether saves about Rs 55.

Rates, charges and taxes

How is a net-metering bill calculated?

Net units per band, plus carried-forward units at settlement; peak units × peak rate and off-peak units × off-peak rate (or the buyback rate if a surplus); plus the fixed charge; plus GST at 18% (and electricity duty if still shown). Arrears or credit are then applied.

Read: How a net-metering bill is calculated, step by step
What is the net-metering buyback rate in 2026?

Rs 25.32 per unit for pre-February 2026 agreements (national average power purchase price). New net-billing prosumers get Rs 8.13 (national average energy purchase price), often reported as Rs 10–11.

How is the fixed charge calculated?

Time-of-use consumers pay Rs 675 per kW per month on the higher of half the sanctioned load or the MDI. A 5 kW connection pays at least Rs 1,687.50, plus GST. Other domestic consumers pay Rs 200–675 per kW on their sanctioned load, depending on usage.

What is FPA on my bill?

Fuel price adjustment: the difference between actual and reference fuel cost for a month, billed per unit two months later. The July 2026 adjustment was +Rs 2.06 per unit in September bills.

What is QTA on my bill?

Quarterly tariff adjustment, decided by NEPRA each quarter and spread over three months of bills. September to November 2026 is +Rs 0.52 per unit.

Is GST charged on net units or all imported units?

A February 2025 Federal Tax Ombudsman order told DISCOs to charge 18% GST on the gross value of imported units. Application varies; many bills still show GST on the net amount.

Is electricity duty still charged?

Not for most domestic consumers since 1 July 2025, when provinces were asked to stop collecting it through bills. Some older or non-domestic bills still show it.

Is the PTV fee still on electricity bills?

No. The Rs 35 PTV fee was removed from July 2025.

Why is income tax charged on my electricity bill?

Domestic consumers not on the FBR Active Taxpayers List pay 7.5% when the monthly bill is Rs 25,000 or more. Becoming a filer with the meter in your name removes it.

Can a solar or net-metering consumer be protected?

Usually not. Time-of-use connections (5 kW and above), which most solar homes have, cannot be protected consumers.

What is the 2026 per-unit tariff for unprotected domestic consumers?

From Rs 22.44 (1–100 units) to Rs 47.20 (over 700 units) per unit under NEPRA’s February 2026 tariff, plus a fixed charge per kW. New rates for 2027 are being set after hearings in October 2026.

Settlement and credit

What does MONTH COUNT 3/3 mean?

This bill is the third and final month of the settlement cycle. Units carried from earlier months are added and priced, then the carry-forward resets.

Read: Quarterly settlement explained: MONTH COUNT, carried-forward units and credit
What are REMAINING-PREVIOUS and REMAINING-PRESENT units?

REMAINING-PREVIOUS is net units carried into this bill from earlier in the cycle; REMAINING-PRESENT is what carries out to next month. A minus sign is a surplus.

Read: Quarterly settlement explained: MONTH COUNT, carried-forward units and credit
Does the DISCO pay me for surplus units?

A net surplus is credited to the next cycle or paid quarterly at the buyback rate. In practice most households see it as a CR balance used against later bills.

Why is my credit balance going down?

Each month’s bill is taken out of the credit. In winter, with fewer exports, the credit is drawn down faster. That is the credit being used, not lost.

2026 rules

Did net metering end in Pakistan?

For new applicants, from 9 February 2026, when NEPRA’s Prosumer Regulations replaced it with net billing. Existing agreements keep the old terms until they expire.

Read: Net metering vs net billing: what changed for solar users in 2026
What is the difference between net metering and net billing?

Net metering nets units (import minus export) and buys a surplus at Rs 25.32. Net billing charges every imported unit at the full tariff and pays Rs 8.13 for every exported unit, settled monthly in rupees.

Am I on net metering or net billing?

Net metering if your agreement was signed before 9 February 2026 or your application was submitted by 8 February 2026; net billing otherwise.

What happens when my net-metering agreement expires?

Renewal is under the 2026 regulations: net billing with 5-year agreements, renewable by mutual consent.

Will I lose net metering if I add panels?

Yes. Expanding capacity after 9 February 2026 moves the connection to net billing.

How big a solar system can I install under the 2026 rules?

Up to your sanctioned load (previously 1.5 times), on a three-phase connection, and only if solar on your transformer is below 80% of its rating.

Do I need a NEPRA licence for rooftop solar?

Not for systems up to 25 kW since April 2026; the DISCO approves them. Larger systems pay a one-time Rs 1,000 per kW.

About 456,000 to 466,000 with 6–7 GW of net-metered solar as of February 2026. LESCO leads with over 135,000 connections and 2,053 MW (May 2026).

Problems and complaints

My export units are not adjusted. What do I do?

Check the export registers and that present minus previous equals units, compare with your inverter’s export, then complain via 118, SMS 8118 or ccms@pitc.com.pk with meter photos. Escalate to NEPRA if unresolved.

How do I complain to NEPRA?

Complain to your DISCO first, then file on NEPRA’s online consumer complaint register with your reference number, bill, evidence and DISCO complaint number.

Can my DISCO bill me for more than 30 days?

No. Meters must be read monthly; NEPRA’s 2023 inquiry treated longer billing periods as a violation.

Photograph your meter readings with the date and complain to the DISCO. The government’s Power Smart app lets you record your own reading.

My green meter is not installed. Who do I contact?

Your DISCO, with your demand-notice receipt. Cases with demand notices paid before 9 February 2026 must be activated on the old terms. Escalate to NEPRA if stuck.

A charge for units the DISCO believes were used but not recorded. You can contest it with the DISCO and then NEPRA; ask for the detection proforma and its basis.
