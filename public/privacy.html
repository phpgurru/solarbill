Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Solar Bill reads your electricity bill inside your browser. If you don’t sign in, your bill never reaches us. If you do, we keep your bills so you can come back to them, and nothing else. We don’t sell data, show ads or use tracking.

1. Who we are

Solar Bill (“we”, “us”) runs the website at solarbill.pk, a free tool that explains Pakistani net-metering electricity bills. Solar Bill is independent. It is not part of, or affiliated with, NEPRA, PITC or any electricity distribution company (DISCO).

2. Using Solar Bill without an account

When you add a bill without signing in, the PDF or photo is opened and read by code running in your own browser. The file and the figures read from it are not sent to us. Closing or reloading the page clears them.

If you start signing in after adding bills, we keep those bills in your browser’s own storage (IndexedDB) for the moment it takes Google to sign you in, so they can be saved to your new account. They are removed from the browser as soon as they are saved.

Like any website, our hosting provider receives basic technical information when your browser loads a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. See section 5.

3. What we store when you sign in

You sign in with your Google account. We ask Google only for your email address and a stable account identifier. We never see your Google password, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Your bill shows your name and address. We don’t read, extract or store them as separate fields, but they remain inside the PDF you save.

4. How we use your information

  • To read your bills and show you the report.
  • To keep your bills in your account, filed by meter.
  • To send a monthly reminder when your next bill should be out, if you leave reminders on.
  • To keep the service secure and working, for example by investigating errors.
  • To count totals for running the service, such as how many accounts and bills exist. These counts don’t identify anyone.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of you. We do not use your bills to train AI models.

7. How long we keep it

  • Bills and PDFs: until you delete them, delete the meter they belong to, or delete your account.
  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Sign-in sessions: 60 days, then removed.
  • Request logs: kept by Cloudflare for a short period, typically days, for security and troubleshooting.

When you delete your account, we remove your bills, PDFs, meters and sessions straight away. Our database provider keeps a rolling recovery copy for up to 30 days, so deleted rows may remain in that copy until it expires. We don’t restore from it except to recover from a failure.

8. Your choices and rights

From My account you can, at any time:

  • See and download everything we hold about you, as a JSON file with links to your PDFs.
  • Correct meter names, or replace a bill by adding it again.
  • Delete single bills, whole meters, or your entire account.
  • Turn off reminders. Every reminder email also has a one-click unsubscribe link.

You can also remove Solar Bill’s access from your Google account under Security, then Third-party apps and services. For anything else, email us (see section 12).

9. Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. PDFs are kept in private storage that only your signed-in account can open. Session identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes, and sign-in uses Google’s OAuth with protection against forgery. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users about any breach as the law requires.

10. Children

Solar Bill is meant for bill payers and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell signed-in users by email or in the app before it takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: hello@solarbill.pk. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Bills are read inside your browser. When you sign in, each PDF and its figures are saved privately to your account, and you can download or delete them any time. Solar Bill is an independent tool, not affiliated with NEPRA, PITC or any electricity distribution company.
