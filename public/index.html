The labels in your bill’s net-metering box and QR code, in one line each.

Import unitsIMP
Electricity you took from the grid, mostly at night and in the evening.
Export unitsEXP
Surplus solar your panels sent to the grid during the day.
Off-peakOP
Every hour outside the evening peak window. Charged, and credited, at the lower rate.
PeakPK
A four-hour evening window, roughly 5–9 PM in winter to 7–11 PM in summer, charged at the higher rate.
Net unitsNET
Imports minus exports in each band. A minus sign means you sent more than you took.
Month count3/3
Where this bill sits in the settlement cycle. Carried units are settled at the end of it.
Carried-forward unitsREMAINING
Surplus units rolled over from earlier months of the cycle.

What the DISCO pays for surplus units. Reported at about Rs 25–26 for pre-2026 agreements.
Green meter
The bidirectional meter fitted for net metering. It records imports and exports separately.
Net billing
The 2026 system for new solar users: imports and exports are priced separately instead of netted.

Net metering in 2026: what changed.

NEPRA’s Prosumer Regulations changed the rules for new rooftop-solar users. Here is what that means for the bill in your hand.

9 February 2026
Net billing replaces net metering for new applicants. Imports are billed at the full tariff and exports are bought back separately, at roughly Rs 8–11 per unit. New agreements run five years, and solar capacity is capped at your sanctioned load. (Dawn, Express Tribune)
April 2026
Existing users keep their terms. Consumers with a valid agreement on 9 February 2026 are billed under the earlier mechanism until it expires. Enlarging the system gives those terms up. (Profit)
466,000 consumers
Pakistan had roughly 466,000 net-metering consumers and about 7,000 MW of grid-tied solar as of February 2026. (Profit)

FAQ

Solar bill questions, answered.

Why did I get a bill when I exported more units than I imported?

Net metering is settled by value, not just units. Evening (peak) imports are charged at the peak rate, while surplus exports are credited at the off-peak rate, and fixed charges, fuel price adjustment and taxes are not netted against exports. So a household can export more units than it uses and still owe money.

On our sample LESCO bill, the quarter ended 165 units in credit but still carried an energy charge of Rs 16,233.

What are import and export units on a net-metering bill?

Import units are the electricity you draw from the grid, mostly at night and in the evening. Export units are the surplus your solar panels send to the grid during the day. Both are recorded by the bidirectional (green) meter and split into peak (PK) and off-peak (OP) registers.

Did net metering end in Pakistan in 2026?

For new applicants, yes. NEPRA’s Prosumer Regulations, notified on 9 February 2026, replaced net metering with net billing, where imports and exports are priced separately.

An amendment notified in April 2026 lets consumers with a valid agreement on 9 February 2026 keep their earlier terms until the agreement expires, as long as they do not enlarge their system.

What is the net-metering buyback rate in 2026?

Reports put the rate for existing (pre-February 2026) agreements at about Rs 25 to 26 per unit, and the rate for new net-billing prosumers at roughly Rs 8 to 11 per unit, linked to the national average energy purchase price. Your own bill shows the rates actually applied to you, and Solar Bill reads them for you.

What are peak hours on an electricity bill?

Peak hours are a four-hour evening window when electricity costs more. The window moves with the season, roughly 5 to 9 PM in winter and 7 to 11 PM in summer. Units you import in this window are billed at the peak rate.

What does MONTH COUNT 3/3 mean on my bill?

It shows where your bill falls in the settlement cycle. Surplus units are carried forward from month to month, and at the end of the cycle (3/3 on a quarterly cycle) the carried units are settled. That is why a bill at the end of a cycle can look very different from the months before it.
