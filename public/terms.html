2. What Solar Bill does

Solar Bill reads net-metering web bills issued by Pakistani electricity distribution companies (DISCOs) and explains them: units imported and exported, peak and off-peak costs, settlement, credit and estimated savings. You can use it without an account, or sign in with Google to save your bills.

Solar Bill is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for NEPRA, PITC or any DISCO, and it cannot change, dispute or pay your bill.

3. Figures are for information

Solar Bill works from what is printed on, and encoded in, your bill. It recomputes figures and adds estimates, such as savings and payback, based on assumptions you can see and change. We work to make it accurate, but:

  • a bill may be read incorrectly, for example if the PDF is damaged, a photo is unclear, or a DISCO changes its layout;
  • tariffs, taxes and regulations change, and our explanations may lag behind them;
  • estimates are not predictions of what you will actually pay or save.

Your DISCO’s bill is the official record. Solar Bill is not financial, legal, tax or engineering advice. Before paying, disputing a bill, or deciding about a solar system, check with your DISCO or a qualified professional. Policy information on our website comes from public sources we link to, and may not reflect the terms of your own agreement.
